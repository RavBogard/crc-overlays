# Renderer fidelity boundary

The renderer implements refreshed CRC lower-third, left-panel, and right-panel geometry at a 1920 × 1080 design size. The visual language uses the CRC-owned Siona Benjamin floor artwork as a circular emblem, with a restrained palette drawn from it: deep blue structure, turquoise accents, warm gold rings, and calm high-contrast prayer surfaces. The detailed artwork stays inside the emblem rather than behind prayer text. The lower third occupies about 29% of the frame and places transliteration and Hebrew side by side.

A fourth layout, `corner`, is a small card for a line or two ("Vaimru Amen", a name said for healing, a one-line thank-you), replacing the old Singular bottom-right pop-up. It sits flush in the bottom-right corner at the same 48 px inset as the resting logo (`RESTING_LOGO_RECT`) and the scan card (`BUG_RESERVED_RECT`): 640 × 200 px at `{left:1232, top:832, right:1872, bottom:1032}`, a 56 px title strip with the flat logo at its right end, then Hebrew (RTL, right-aligned) above its transliteration (left-aligned), or one lone channel centred in the body. The card is drawn from its definition (`CORNER_CARD` in `lib/layout-registry.ts`, read by the generic `.overlay[data-card]` block in `app/overlay.css`), and its fit strategy (`shrink-to-floor`) shrinks a line that does not fit its box down to `CORNER_FONT_FLOOR` (20 px) and marks the box `data-fit="overflow"` if it still does not. The catalog has no corner baseline cue: a corner draft takes a lower-third template for its motion and duration (`templateLayoutFor` in `lib/layout-label.ts`), with the bars scaling in from the card's right edge. A corner card cannot be cut into a set of slides (`corner_set_unsupported`) and does not take a lit Translation layer (`corner_translation_unsupported`). Like every cue it suppresses the resting logo while it holds the stage, and it covers the scan card, which paints beneath the graphics (below).

Animation tracks preserve archive timing offsets and power-easing intent where current cues provide them. Fade, `scaleX`, `scaleY`, two-axis scale, and up/down/left/right translate effects are supported. Known `logoGroup`, `titleGroup`, `HebText`, and `EngText` tracks are applied in addition to direct tile tracks. This is a bounded mapping of CRC cue behavior, not a generic Singular renderer or a pixel-equivalent recreation.

Existing cue fields remain valid. `template` is optional and additive: `{ family?: "lower-third" | "panel-left" | "panel-right", version?: string, translatePx?: number }`. A cue with `textMain` renders that curated combined block. Otherwise `textMainEng` and `textMainheb` render as separate blocks; the renderer does not copy or synthesize text between them. `accentTextTitle` is rendered when supplied.

Both archived Work Sans files are declared as weights 400 and 500. The archive does not identify the production Hebrew fallback, so Hebrew uses the browser fallback chain and exact Hebrew font equivalence remains unverified. Transparent output is preserved. A cut cancels active Web Animations and clears the output immediately; stale or duplicate revisions cannot replace a newer requested state.

The production overlay faces today are WorkRefresh (Latin) and Noto Sans Hebrew (Hebrew/RTL), declared in `app/globals.css` with `font-display:block` so a fit measurement never runs against a fallback glyph metric. A second, trial pairing — David Libre and Frank Ruhl Libre, both from Google Fonts' OFL-licensed `google/fonts` repository — lives behind `app/overlay-faces.css` and only applies to elements carrying a `faces-book` class; it is enabled deployment-wide via `WORKSPACE_BOOK_FACES=1` (`lib/workspace.ts` `PublicWorkspace.bookFaces`, default off — see `docs/REHEARSAL-MODE.md` for the rehearsal `--book-faces` flag). `lib/overlay-assets.ts` `waitForOverlayFonts`/`waitForRenderedOverlayAssets` accept a `faces: 'default' | 'book'` argument so a book-faces render waits on the additional faces before fitting. The OFL license text for every bundled font sits beside it in `public/assets/*-OFL.txt`. `/output` and the console's local inspect preview both toggle the `faces-book` class and the `faces` argument from `workspace.bookFaces`, so nothing changes there while the flag is off. `/author/fit-check/faces` is a read-only compare page: it renders every visible published graphic with both face sets side by side, with fit errors and fitted font sizes under each, plus a query mode (`?cue=&faces=&frame=`) that renders one bare stage and marks it `data-ready="1"` once settled; `scripts/t3-stills.mjs` drives that query mode with `playwright-core` to capture default/book/side-by-side stills for the whole catalog into `work/handoffs/phase-b/t3/`.

## The scan-card layer

`/output` renders a second stage, `#output-bug`, as a **sibling** of `#output` and not a child of it. `lib/player.ts` owns `#output` and calls `root.replaceChildren()` on every cue change; a card inside that root would be destroyed by the next graphic, so it is kept outside and driven separately by `lib/bug-layer.ts` (`renderBugLayer(root, view)`, idempotent, `null` clears). Both stages take the same `scale()` from the existing size handler, so the card tracks the 1920 × 1080 design size exactly as the graphics do.

The card paints **under** the graphics. `#output-bug` is the earlier sibling and carries no `z-index`, so a Right panel, Lower third or Corner card whose box reaches the corner covers it and prayer text is never occluded; the card shows through wherever the graphic is transparent.

The reserved rectangle is fixed in stage coordinates and exported as `BUG_RESERVED_RECT` from `lib/bug-layer.ts`: 300 × 300 px inset 48 px from the right and bottom edges, i.e. `{left:1572, top:732, right:1872, bottom:1032}`. `app/author/preview.ts` `findBugCollisions` measures published graphics against that rectangle with the same `FONT_METRIC_TOLERANCE`-scaled slack the overlap checker uses, and `/author/fit-check` reports it in a **Scan card corner** column.

The QR image is served by `GET /api/bug/qr.svg` — public, no credential, `image/svg+xml`, `Cache-Control: public, max-age=86400, stale-while-revalidate=604800` — encoded on the server from the workspace's configured card URL. Changing where the card points is a configuration edit, not a render change or a re-cut graphic.

The layer is **absent when the workspace has no card**: with no configured URL the card is not rendered, the console control is not shown, `/api/command` refuses `action:'bug'`, and `/api/bug/qr.svg` answers `404`. `app/bug-layer.css` carries the card's own appearance (parchment `#fbf7ef`, gold rule `#bd8a2e`, ink `#1b1b24`); nothing about it reaches `lib/player.ts`, so no published graphic changes behaviour.
